# Building research handoff

Keep A0 as the landscape and density control. Carry the 2,200 m A1 crop as the leading composition candidate and recheck its urban share after receiving official heights. Use A2 for comparing low-rise silhouette, changes of roofline, wall fragments and contact with sloped terrain. The A2 balance is local and estimates provide 74 of its 100 visible masses. Avoid treating it as a confirmed depiction of Seoul building heights.

- **Apartment blocks** have enough native height support to begin a limited silhouette, stack and plane-break study. Check joined wings, phase grouping and parallel blocks against the matched footprint examples.
- **Houses and detached buildings** can enter the size test using the separate estimate scenario. Build a handpicked sample with verified height, roof and use before defining a catalog from them.
- **Low-rise commercial and neighborhood facilities** have enough visible small forms for spacing tests. Seoul's official neighborhood-use classes remain pending. Keep the modeled shop-like forms separate from buildings with confirmed use.
- **Other or missing use** may support morphology tests based on shape. Leave the original classification and unknown height visible in the metadata.

The smallest next input that unlocks H1 is the Seoul VWorld GIS Integrated Building Information ZIP with its SHP, SHX, DBF and PRJ and any provider dictionary. It has joined attributes and geometry in one bundle. If needed, link Seoul or Building HUB title data through a verified common ID and then compare address and parcel records for mismatches. Do not require an API pipeline when a bulk file is available.

After receiving data, run the official provider on locked A0 before adjusting A1 and A2. Compare district, vintage and use coverage, audit asymmetric matches and use conflicts, and inspect roof heights and footprints matched to each other. Refine crop boundaries after measuring space left by newly modeled low-rise buildings, especially where building masses interrupt prior A/D terrain marks. Keep the terrain base policy and provisional near and middle visibility limits. Confirm a terrain license and a fine, comparable datum for an eventual commercial work.
