The actual v3 GeoPackage fields were inspected. In addition to its geometry and fid, the file contains `ArcheType`, `ResidFloorArea_gf_m2`, `Stock_GF_Resid`, `Stock_GF_Steel_Resid`, `EC_GF_Resid`, `EC_GF_Concrete_Resid`, `EC_GF_Steel_Resid`, `GrossFloorArea_gf_m2` and `Height_gf_m`. The catalogue both describes a gap-filled height field and states that building heights are excluded for licensing reasons. No official-versus-modeled indicator exists for the field. The workflow reads only fid, geometry and archetype. The download matches the catalogue's MD5 and the provider manifest pins its SHA-256.

Published Overture tables use ODbL 1.0. The research footprint source is kept separate and credited in derived comparisons. See [Overture attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/) and [ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). No raw GeoPackage is distributed with this checkpoint.

FABDEM v1.2 stays on the prior non-commercial research footing. An appropriately licensed fine national [NGII DEM](https://www.data.go.kr/data/15059920/fileData.do) is the first local terrain candidate. The catalogue lists unrestricted use, but the actual public Seoul tiles, datum, effective grid and availability through the download client still need verification. [MERIT DEM](https://global-hydrodynamics.github.io/MERIT_DEM/) offers an ODbL route and may be suitable for a broad ridge comparison at roughly 90 m. Its EGM96 datum and cell size make it a poor direct substitute for local building contacts. Neither alternative is applied to Work 01B.
