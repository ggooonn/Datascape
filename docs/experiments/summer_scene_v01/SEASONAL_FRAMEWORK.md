# Eight-panel seasonal framework / provisional v01

P1–P8 each measure 300 × 900 mm. Each seasonal pair is a continuous 600 × 900 mm painting; installation adds a 40 mm gap between every panel without stretching or deleting painted content. The horizontal sequence describes seasonal change, not a continuous geographic survey. Autumn and Winter remain hypotheses, not generated scenes.

The implemented Summer growth modifier is a smoothstep across X=210–390 mm. Its center lies at the P3/P4 boundary, without a geometric or stylistic discontinuity there. Unequal fixed group recipes and measured terrain contacts determine actual counts. P4 need not be uniformly denser than P3, and skyline continuity does not dictate its growth distribution.

## Spring-to-Summer and skyline reading

Use `transition_P1_P4.png` for the actual approved Spring raster beside actual Summer vectors. Spring receives no repainting, silhouette correction, new objects or new colors. P3 inherits a readable low ground and open cluster spacing; P4 grows an unequal right grove and higher mountain sequence.

`skyline_candidates_P1_P4.png` compares the **terrain** silhouettes before mist, vegetation and display styling. Values use the same top-down millimeters with no vertical fitting. The plotted curves must break at physical gaps. Boundary height and 35 mm end-window slopes are diagnostics, not an objective score or a connection claim.

| Candidate | P3 start minus Spring P2 end | Assessment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A / southern core approach | −55 mm | Two high side masses, intervening saddle; independent but stronger height step |
| B / northwest toward B1 | −111 mm | Strong valley opening and broad descending ridge; useful alternative but greater boundary reset |
| C / eastern transition | −18 mm | Closest unfitted height; rising right summit contrasts with descending Spring; provisionally selected |

C reverses the immediate boundary slope (Spring +0.424 versus Summer −0.653 mm/mm in top-down coordinates). The seam therefore reads as a shallow receiving hollow, not an exactly continuing tangent. This is a visible unresolved artistic question; the principle remains soft. Do not translate or deform either scene to erase it. The original A1/B1/C1 bounds and camera records remain in PR4's unchanged exploration JSON; new approaches use a wider existing DEM window to retain foreground coverage, not geographic tile relocation.
