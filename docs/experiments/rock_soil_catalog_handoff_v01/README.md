## Next catalog scope

Focus on foreground and middle-ground structures. These are **roles to study**,
not a requirement to build four independent generators or a broad species list.

| Role | Required distinction | Shared interface contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opaque foreground rock mass | Solid visible mass versus its drawn contour/surface strokes | Visible opaque body, local depth, footprint-safe hard exclusion and controlled outer ground relationship |
| Exposed bedrock / broken rock surface | Surface-following exposed structure, breaks and interruptions versus a freestanding opaque mass | Surface ownership, visible breaks, optional bounded body regions; no automatic whole-support opacity |
| Soil / exposed ground, slopes and small mounds | Surface texture and local ground masses that replace, reinforce or selectively omit existing A/C strokes | Explicit replace/reinforce/omit intent, source stroke/arc references, ground support and duplicate-coverage accounting |
| Soil–rock–root interface | Contact, partial burial, exposed root and retained supporting ground | Contact surface/depth, protected stroke intervals, local transition and unresolved attachment flags |

Keep these ink vectors distinct from later pigment support and measured brush
execution. A soil patch should not inherit an opaque-rock mask by default, and
a broken rock outline should not make every gap transparent by default. Declare
the intended structure and test it locally.

## Open pictorial corridors — organize negative space first

Treat these as a separate spatial concept shared by terrain and object placement,
not another material family or an instruction to generate more marks:

- Valley-floor openings and low passages between ground masses.
- Possible stream/waterside passages, without asserting mapped water.
- Optional footpath-like gaps, without asserting a human route.
- Spatial whitespace that must not automatically be filled by rock, soil
  texture, replacement A/C strokes or vegetation.

Record a corridor's stable ID, intended opening/support geometry, local depth
or terrain relation, evidence source, uncertainty, allowed minimal edge marks
and review status. A DEM valley is evidence of terrain form only; it does not
prove a stream or a footpath. A pictorial corridor is not a new global F2 mist
field. Preserve its separate omission/placement reason and respect the locked
global whitespace and bottom guard.

Start by arranging rock/ground masses around an opening and, only where needed,
using a few supporting boundary marks. Keep the opening readable in black ink
and in grouped-object conditions. Test that object/texture proposals do not
automatically refill it, while selected edge/root supports remain possible.
Do not impose explicit river, water or path generators at the outset. Propose
such strokes only if later visual tests show that negative space plus minimal
boundary marks cannot communicate the intended passage; keep any resulting
water/path interpretation explicitly hypothetical unless independently sourced.

## Limited interaction proofs for the next catalog

These are future comparisons, **not outputs claimed by this handoff**. Reuse
existing v10 trees without modifying their grammar. Keep seeds, tree geometry,
test strokes, physical scale and assumed brush footprint matched within each
comparison. Terrain crossing probes remain explicitly synthetic.

1. **Rock body / terrain:** no exclusion, excessive support masking and proposed
   shared body/transition behavior; include one nearer-depth crossing.
2. **Tree canopy / strong terrain:** v10 selective proof versus broader occupied
   core plus transition release; keep a full-support control to expose white
   holes. Review a selected foreground tree and a smaller middle-ground group
   with explicit, separately recorded pictorial policies.
3. **Tree root / rock / soil:** compare exposed and partly hidden contact with
   specific supporting stroke intervals retained. Include a conflict where a
   protected stroke would cross the trunk body; do not quietly waive depth.
4. **Overlapping objects:** tree in front of rock and rock in front of tree;
   hidden parts must not keep masking terrain. Reverse processing traversal
   while preserving depth/IDs to check determinism rather than draw-order tricks.
5. **Ground replacement / reinforcement:** synthetic A/C-like source paths with
   explicit lineage, not regenerated baseline A/C. Demonstrate replacement and
   reinforcement separately, including the duplicate-ink failure case.
6. **Open corridor / grouped masses:** compare the same rocks, ground texture
   and existing trees with an opening respected versus automatically refilled.
   Try negative space alone, then minimal boundary cues. No explicit water/path
   grammar unless this later comparison demonstrates a need.

Report actual kept/deleted lengths, affected source paths, output fragments,
post-simplification minimum lengths, preserved contact intervals and footprint
assumptions. Inspect the black-vector result and physical-size preview alongside
those measurements. Less deleted length or fewer fragments alone is not a
pictorial acceptance criterion. Do not use raster transparency as the proof.

## Stop conditions and later acceptance

The next catalog can close provisionally when its compact rock/soil grammar is
readable, reproducible and provides compatible interaction intent. It does not
need to solve full reciprocal masking first. Carry OCC-01 through OCC-08 from
the shared contract forward with explicit statuses and evidence.

**Later scene integration**, after rock/soil grammar acceptance, must resolve
actual terrain-relative placement and depth, mutually visible tree/rock masks,
canopy core/transitional suppression, selected root support, unchanged global
mist/whitespace and executable final fragments. **Physical plotting approval**
additionally requires measured footprints, tolerances, drying, ink/pigment
sequencing and paper behavior. No such acceptance is granted by this handoff.
