# Final decisions and unresolved questions

The user's revised v05 closure direction governs this handoff. Earlier visual
notes are retained as history, not approval. Technical validation does not
promote a pictorial candidate.

## Retain

- R/S/U-style mid/far grammar is the primary reusable result: outward crowns,
  shared multi-body canopy carriers and jointed leader/lateral growth.
- Keep terrain x/source-q support, unequal group roles, territorial extent
  versus cluster concentration, independent ink/color construction, original
  arc provenance, protected reserves, actual source-depth mist selection and
  discrete existing-palette material weighting.
- Keep B only as a far-distance optional provisional candidate. Middle distance
  continues primarily with R/S/U and the existing mid/far vocabulary.
- B's future ink and color must relate continuously across shared passages.
  Color is a coherent far vegetation layer, not a disconnected overlay.

## Defer

- v05 A and C are not accepted general-use solutions. Do not adopt C merely
  because the prior review suggested it as a next starting point.
- Do not resume broad large-forest-mass refinement in this Work.
- B is not a fully accepted grammar, not the middle-distance recommendation,
  and not a calibrated brush-plotter primitive set. Historical color dots and
  medium-grove ink do not constitute the required linked ink/color behavior.
- Do not confuse the vegetation family names R/S/U with accepted Tree v10
  P/B/U or Rock & Ground formation A/B. Their meanings and namespaces differ.

## Unresolved for the next Spring integration

| Issue | Required next-stage examination |
| --- | --- |
| Large vegetation continuity | Convincing slope/ridge fields, dominant/subordinate masses and open intervals; current carriers can remain parallel hatching or small crown signs |
| Middle versus far | Preserve readable middle growth structure while far texture acts as subordinate terrain grain; prevent historical middle B from leaking into defaults |
| Linked B ink and color | Shared passage IDs/supports, growth/terrain direction, related density and mist omission; no unrelated scatter or color-only add-on masquerading as a paired result |
| Small physical marks | 0.9-mm dots, tight turns, short marks, contact/Z/dwell/loading/pressure/spread and material accumulation remain uncalibrated |
| Depth transitions | Mist entry/reappearance, relief and occlusion, changing density, source-q along long carriers and clearings must work together |
| Overall Spring color | Far plant material must relate to A-displaced terrain color, C-guided color and woody/ground washes without making foreground contrast or middle density collapse |
| General integration | Temporary scene placements, native scale, reciprocal object/terrain visibility, root/ground contact, weak C/color density and woody wash remain open from the Spring checkpoint |

Existing 20-color HEX values and stations are unchanged. The v04 material
weights are reusable experiments, not a resolved full-Spring color system.
Source-over display alpha is not physical pigment mixing. Do not fix mist
solely by fading opacity or declare forest success from path counts.

Continuous ink >=2.8 mm and color >=3.2 mm retain their existing floors.
Experimental point contacts are typed separately; they are not zero-length
strokes claimed to meet those floors. A compact tuft may meet path length
while still merging under the brush. No hardware-ready claim follows.
